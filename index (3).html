Questions

Before you book.

What is a 360° walk-in tour?

It is a set of 360° photos, one for each room, joined together. The viewer drags to look around a room and taps a marker to move to the next one, as in the demo at the top of this page.

How should the property be prepared?

Tidy surfaces, open the curtains, turn on every light and move cars off the drive. For hotels, have the room made up as a guest would find it.

How do I add the tour to my listing?

You get a web link. Paste it into the virtual tour field on the property portal or add it to your own website.

Can you fly a drone at my property?

In many places, yes. Parts of London have airspace limits, so I check the location before the shoot and tell you if a flight is allowed. Drone shoots also need dry, calm weather.

What if I need to change the day?

Send a new request for the new day and mention the change in the notes. I'll confirm the new day.
